# Support Article: Diagnosing slow flow on FS-500 units

**Document:** KB-0142
**Revision:** 2
**Updated:** 2024-04-30
**Audience:** First-line support

---

## Symptom

The customer reports that water dispenses more slowly than it used to, or that
filling a bottle now takes noticeably longer.

## Expected rate

A healthy FS-500 dispenses at 1.8 L/min. Below roughly 1.2 L/min the firmware
raises a low flow condition and the unit reports it to the fleet service.

Ask the customer to time a one-litre fill before escalating. A fill taking
longer than 50 seconds is worth a visit; a fill under 35 seconds is within
normal range and the complaint is more likely about perceived wait than about
flow.

## Common causes

**Exhausted sediment filter.** By far the most frequent cause. A sediment
filter at end of life restricts the whole path, and the symptom appears
gradually over weeks rather than suddenly.

**Exhausted carbon filter.** Less common as a flow restriction, but a carbon
filter well past its interval will contribute, and is usually accompanied by a
taste complaint.

**Low supply pressure.** Check whether other outlets on the same supply are
affected. Building works and mains repairs are a frequent cause and resolve
themselves.

**Partially closed isolation valve.** Common after any nearby plumbing work.
Worth asking about before booking a visit.

## What not to do

Do not book a carbonator inspection for a slow flow complaint on a sparkling
unit. Carbonation and dispense rate are independent; a flat sparkling complaint
and a slow flow complaint have no cause in common.

## Escalation

If the filters are within interval and supply pressure is normal, raise a
repair with the timed fill result attached.
