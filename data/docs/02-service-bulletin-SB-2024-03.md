# Service Bulletin SB-2024-03

**Subject:** Revised carbon filter interval, FS-500 series
**Issued:** 2024-02-09
**Supersedes:** none
**Action:** Mandatory at next scheduled visit

---

## Summary

Field data collected across the FS-500 fleet during 2023 shows carbon filter
performance falling below specification before the published interval is
reached. Effective immediately, the carbon filter replacement interval is
reduced to 4 months on all FS-500 series units.

Engineers should not wait for a taste complaint before acting on this bulletin.

## Affected units

All FS-500-STL, FS-500-CHL and FS-500-SPK units, regardless of installation
date or region.

## Revised interval

| Consumable | Previous | Revised |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Carbon filter | 6 months | 4 months |

The sediment filter interval is unchanged at 12 months. The UV lamp interval is
unchanged at 9,000 operating hours.

## Procedure

1. Isolate the mains supply and vent the line.
2. Remove and dispose of the exhausted carbon filter through the cartridge
   take-back scheme. Do not place it in general waste.
3. Fit the replacement cartridge and flush 5 litres to waste.
4. Record the filter replacement against the unit serial number.

## Note for FS-500-SPK units

On sparkling units the lower cabinet is shared between the filter manifold and
the gas bottle. Close the regulator on the gas bottle before working on the
manifold, and confirm the gauge has fallen to zero before breaking any
connection.

Several engineers have reported difficulty reaching the right-hand manifold
position with a full-height gas bottle fitted. Where access is restricted,
remove the gas bottle first and refit it after the cartridge change is
complete. Do not leave a partially seated gas bottle in the retaining bracket
overnight.

## Reporting

Log the visit against the unit and note the litres dispensed since the previous
filter replacement, which is shown on the service screen.
