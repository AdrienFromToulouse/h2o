## About your dispenser

**Q. What is the difference between still, chilled and sparkling?**

Still water is filtered and dispensed at mains temperature. Chilled water passes
through the chiller block first. Sparkling water is chilled and then carbonated.
Not every model dispenses all three; check the label inside the lower door.

**Q. Where does the water come from?**

Directly from your mains supply. The dispenser filters, treats and, depending on
the model, chills or carbonates it on demand. There is no bottle to change and
no delivery to schedule.

## Consumables and service

**Q. Do I need to change the filter myself?**

No. Filter replacement is carried out by an engineer as part of your service
contract. You do not need to order cartridges or keep spares.

**Q. My sparkling water has gone flat. What should I do?**

This usually means the gas bottle needs exchanging. Raise a service request and
an engineer will bring a replacement. Please do not attempt to change the gas
bottle yourself: the connection is pressurised and requires a leak test after
refitting.

**Q. Can I keep a spare gas bottle in the office?**

We do not recommend it. Cylinders must be stored upright, secured and away from
heat, and most offices have nowhere suitable. Your service schedule is set so
that a spare should not be necessary.

**Q. The water tastes different. Is something wrong?**

A change in taste is most often a sign that the carbon filter is due for
replacement. Raise a service request and note when you first noticed the change.

## Sustainability reporting

**Q. What do the figures on my quarterly report mean?**

Your report shows the volume dispensed, an estimate of the single-use bottles
avoided, and an estimate of the CO₂e avoided. The bottle figure assumes a
500 ml reference bottle; the emissions figure uses a published factor, which is
stated in the report footer. Both are estimates and are labelled as such.

## Faults

**Q. There is water under the unit.**

Treat any leak as urgent. Switch the unit off at the socket, close the isolation
valve if you can reach it safely, and raise a priority service request.

**Q. Nothing happens when I press dispense.**

Check the socket and the isolation valve first. If both are fine, raise a
service request and quote the code shown on the display.
