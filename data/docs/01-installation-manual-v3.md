# FS-500 Series Installation and Commissioning Manual

**Document:** IM-FS500
**Revision:** 3
**Issued:** 2023-04-17
**Applies to:** FS-500-STL, FS-500-CHL, FS-500-SPK

---

## 1. Before you begin

The FS-500 series is a freestanding dispenser intended for permanent connection
to a potable mains supply. Do not connect the unit to a supply that has not been
verified as potable.

Required clearances: 80 mm at the rear, 50 mm on each side. The chiller block
vents rearwards and will trip its thermal cut-out if the rear clearance is
reduced.

## 2. Water connection

Connect the 8 mm inlet to a dedicated isolation valve. The unit requires a
supply pressure between 1.5 and 6.0 bar. Above 6.0 bar an inline pressure
reducer is mandatory; the RO membrane housing is not rated above that figure.

Flush 5 litres to waste before proceeding.

## 3. Fitting the sediment filter and carbon filter

The FS-500 uses a two-stage filtration path. Fit the sediment filter to the
left-hand manifold position and the carbon filter to the right-hand position.
Both cartridges seat with a quarter turn clockwise.

The sediment filter removes suspended particles ahead of the finer stage. The
carbon filter reduces chlorine, taste and odour compounds and must be fitted
after the sediment filter, never before it.

## 4. Fitting the CO₂ cylinder (FS-500-SPK only)

The FS-500-SPK carbonates on demand and is supplied without a charged cylinder.

1. Open the lower cabinet door.
2. Seat the CO₂ cylinder in the retaining bracket and hand-tighten the regulator.
3. Open the regulator valve slowly. The gauge should settle between 8.0 and
   9.5 bar at room temperature.
4. Leak-test the connection with a soap solution before closing the door.

Never transport the unit with a charged CO₂ cylinder fitted.

## 5. UV lamp

The UV lamp is fitted at the factory and is energised whenever the unit is
powered. Do not operate the unit with the UV chamber removed.

## 6. First flush and commissioning

Dispense 10 litres of still water, then 2 litres of chilled water, then, on
FS-500-SPK units, 2 litres of sparkling water. Discard all of it.

Record the commissioning date on the service label inside the lower door.

## 7. Consumable replacement schedule

| Consumable | Interval |
| --- | --- |
| Sediment filter | Every 12 months |
| Carbon filter | Every 6 months, or 4,500 litres, whichever comes first |
| UV lamp | Every 9,000 operating hours |
| Mineral cartridge | Every 12 months |

The carbon filter must be replaced every 6 months. Exceeding this interval
degrades taste performance and is the most common cause of a taste complaint.

## 8. Sanitisation

A full sanitisation of the internal waterways is required every 6 months and
must be recorded against the unit serial number.
